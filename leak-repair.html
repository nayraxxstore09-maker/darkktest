Leak repair

Finding where the water gets in and stopping it.

The stain is not always under the leak

Water can travel along rafters and decking before it shows up inside. The spot on your ceiling is often a few feet away from the actual opening. Finding the real entry point is the most important part of leak repair.

Common causes

  • Failed flashing at chimneys, walls and valleys
  • Cracked vent pipe boots
  • Worn fasteners on metal roofs
  • Damaged or loose panels or roofing material
  • Gaps along edges, ridges and seams

While you wait for a repair

Move belongings away from the wet area, put a bucket under any drip, and take photos of the damage. Photos help us and may help with an insurance claim.

Roof leaking now?

Call us and describe where the water is showing up. The sooner a leak is fixed, the less damage it does to your ceiling, insulation and framing.
