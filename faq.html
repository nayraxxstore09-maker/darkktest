Frequently asked questions

Quick answers. For anything else, call us.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Coleman, Texas. Call and tell us where the property is and we will let you know if we can help.

How do I get an estimate?

Call (325) 770-0900 and describe the job. We will tell you what happens next.

My roof is leaking. What should I do first?

Move belongings out of the way, put a bucket under the drip and take photos of the damage. Then call us so we can find the source.

Do you work on metal roofs?

Yes. We install, repair and seal metal roofs, including replacing worn fasteners.

How often should a roof be inspected?

It is a good idea to have it checked after hail or high wind, and any time you notice stains, rust or loose material.

What causes most roof leaks?

Failed flashing, cracked vent boots, worn fasteners and damaged roofing material are the usual causes.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Photos of the damage help with any claim. Call us to talk about your situation.
