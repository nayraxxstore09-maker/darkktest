Flashing and vents

The details around your roof openings that keep water out.

Flashing

Flashing is the thin metal that seals the joints where the roof meets something else: a wall, a chimney, a skylight or a valley between two slopes. When it rusts, lifts or its sealant dries out, water gets behind it. Flashing problems are one of the most common sources of roof leaks.

  • Repairing or replacing damaged flashing
  • Resealing joints and edges
  • Flashing at chimneys, walls, valleys and edges

Vents

Every pipe and vent that passes through your roof is a possible leak point. The rubber collar around a vent pipe cracks in the sun over time, and the seal around other vents can fail too.

  • Replacing cracked pipe boots
  • Repairing or replacing roof vents
  • Sealing around vents and other penetrations
